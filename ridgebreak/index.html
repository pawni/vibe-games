How to play

  1. Drag across the battlefield to aim and release to fire. You can also use the sliders and Fire volley. The dotted line shows the lead shot’s path. Tap a cache to aim toward it.
  2. Fire to break rock and damage the enemy castle with whatever impact is left. Power-ups take effect the moment you hit them.
  3. Watch for the counterattack. Either side can fire through existing breaches and claim untouched power-ups.
  4. Destroy the entire enemy castle to earn an upgrade. Losing lets you retry the round with your earned upgrades.
